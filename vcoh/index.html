pgls as an edge weight: DBoW2 per sequence (ΔATE, m)

Every pgls weight is worse than weight 1 for DBoW2 and the whole loss is on skatepark. pgls measures “same place”, which is neither necessary nor sufficient for a useful loop edge: use it to label, not to weight.
